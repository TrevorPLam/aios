**Secondary:**

**Strong Moats:**
1. **Ecosystem Lock-in** - 14 integrated modules
2. **Privacy Architecture** - Can't be replicated by cloud-only
3. **Test Coverage** - 100% indicates reliability
4. **Code Quality** - 95-98/100 Perfect Standards

**Build:**
